# Code review, regression lens: poster-tiler

Verdict: the math and the PDF are correct (0 must-fix); 9 should-fix items, mostly wrong or overstated claims and edge cases, plus 6 nice-to-have.

Checked by hand and by code: `npm test` passes. Both article tables recomputed with `layout()` (4 poster sizes x Letter/A4 x portrait/landscape, and pages-across widths 2 to 6 in inches and cm): all 20+ cells match the page. Worked example (24 x 36 in: 4x4 = 16 portrait, 3x5 = 15 landscape) matches. FAQ numbers (3600 px / 24 in = 150 dpi; 8 in banner on landscape Letter = 1 row) match. Exact-fit edges (A4 21 cm and 42 cm, 29.7 cm height, 15.5/23/30.5 in typed in cm) give the right counts. PDF placement was rendered with PyMuPDF using a gradient image where color equals position: all 12 sample points on a 3x2 Letter poster equal the expected poster coordinate, the overlap strips of neighbor tiles are pixel-identical (right/left and bottom/top), margins and the area below the poster are white. Template rules hold: canonical on every page, noindex only on 404 plus `_headers` pages.dev rules, footer links to About/Contact/Privacy/Terms, 404 page, sitemap has 5 URLs without 404, no GA or AdSense script in `dist` when IDs are empty, `ads.txt` is a comment line. Known template leftovers (template README, new-tool.sh, screenshots in git, duplicate legal links on home page, build-date "Last updated", British spelling) are on the fixer's list and not repeated here.
